Signal Processing & Research
MSc Research · TU Ilmenau

Stereo BSS via Unfolded Complex Givens Rotations

Novel approach to Blind Source Separation for stereo audio mixtures using deep unfolding of complex-valued Givens rotations with discriminative training. Separates two concurrent speakers from a two-microphone recording without prior knowledge of the mixing process or speaker characteristics.

Unlike classical ICA, the model learns frequency-dependent rotation angles (θ, φ) through K unfolded iterations in the complex STFT domain. A key challenge was output collapse — where both output channels converge to the mixture mean. Solved with a custom discriminative loss that penalises inter-output correlation. Room acoustics simulated with pyroomacoustics (RT60: 0.1–0.6s); training data from LibriSpeech with 7 augmentation strategies including channel swap, time masking, and additive noise.

SIR: 7–9 dB  ·  SDR: 5–7 dB  ·  SAR: 12–15 dB  ·  ~0.15s per 4.5s mixture (GPU)
